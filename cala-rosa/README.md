# Cala Rosa

A live Three.js miniature of a Mediterranean seaside village, inspired by a reference illustration.

- Terraced houses with tiled roofs, shutters, balconies and chimneys; a plaza with pool, stairs and market props; boulder seawall and animated shoreline water
- Nineteen townsfolk who walk routes, sit, chat, sell, paint and fish; pigeons, gulls, boats, laundry and chimney smoke
- Midday, golden hour and blue hour lighting, tilt-shift depth of field, bloom, orbit and zoom

Open `https://irk15.github.io/3D-/cala-rosa/` after GitHub Pages deploys, or serve this folder over HTTP (`python -m http.server`). Static HTML; Three.js r147 is bundled in `vendor/` with its license.
