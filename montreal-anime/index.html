      How to play

      🕹 Left
      Put your thumb anywhere on the left: joystick. Push to the edge to run.
      👆 Right
      Swipe to turn the camera · pinch with two fingers to zoom.
      ⤒ Jump
      Twice for a flip · three times to take off · hold in the air to glide with the umbrella.
      🕊 Fly
      ▲ Up, ▼ Down, ⚡ Turbo (hold). The camera sets the direction. Wings get tired: land to recharge.
      👆 Bubble
      When a bubble appears on the right (talk, drive, take a BIXI, deliver…), tap it.
      🚗 Car
      Joystick: steer · ⏶ Gas · ⏷ Brake and reverse · ⟲ Handbrake to drift · 👁 interior view.
      🔫 Gun
      Tap Gun to draw · Fire (aim assist) · ⟳ reload · ⇄ opens the weapon wheel. Cars knock people over; the police notice. Grab green ✚ health, blue armour, cash and ammo on the sidewalks. 📻 changes the radio in a car.
      ☰ Menu
      Missions, friends, time, weather, season, journal, photo, music, fullscreen.
      Goal
      Deliver parcels on time, find the 12 golden bagels, win missions against your friends.
      WASD
      Walk · Shift run
      Tab O
      Weapon wheel (time slows) · change radio station
      G U R
      Draw / holster the gun · switch weapon · reload · click to fire · right-click to zoom · K PvP on/off
      Space
      Jump · twice for a flip · hold in the air to glide with the umbrella
      F
      Dash, even in mid-air
      V
      Fly! (or 3rd jump) · Space up · C down · Shift turbo · the camera sets the direction. Wings get tired: land to recharge.
      🚗 E
      Next to a car: drive it (or carjack a moving one). W/S gas and brake, Space handbrake (drifts!), X interior view, B horn, E exit.
      N
      Start a mission: stars, rings, treasure, king of the roof, lightning delivery. First to finish wins!
