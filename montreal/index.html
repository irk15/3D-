      Getting around Montréal

      Orbit (1)
      Drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, WASD to glide. Click a label to learn about it.
      Fly (2)
      Click to capture the mouse. WASD fly, E/Q up and down, Shift faster.
      Walk (3)
      Street level with collisions. WASD, Shift to run, Space to jump. Esc frees the mouse.
      Meta Quest 3
      Open this page in the Quest Browser and press Enter VR. Right stick forward and release to teleport, sideways to snap-turn. Left stick to walk (grip to sprint). Y shows the wrist menu: time, season, weather, landmarks and the map. Point at a landmark and pull the trigger for its story.
