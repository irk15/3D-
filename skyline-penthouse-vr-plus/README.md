# Skyline Penthouse VR Plus

The same penthouse as `../skyline-penthouse-vr/` (that page is unchanged) with a much more detailed city outside:

- **Helicopter**: full fuselage, glass cockpit, tail boom, spinning main and tail rotors, skids, searchlight and strobes
- **Planes**: swept wings, engines, airline-coloured tails, wing lights and contrails
- **Cars**: detailed bodies with glass cabins and wheels, plus SUVs, vans and buses
- **Boats**: yachts, a cargo ship, a ferry and a tanker, each with a foam wake
- **City extras**: rooftop steam plumes and animated LED billboards

Pictures, videos and wallpapers use the files in `paintings/` (see `paintings/README.txt`). Controls are the same as the standard VR page.
