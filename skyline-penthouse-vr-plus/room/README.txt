THE VAULT - your content
========================
Edit these files on GitHub (skyline-penthouse-vr-plus/room/). Each one is a list of links or file names.

posters.json   digital posters (8 of them). Tap a poster in the vault to swap it.
               ["https://.../pic1.jpg", {"src":"pic2.jpg","title":"Sunset","price":"$120"}]
               Use "title" and "price" to make it a little shop: they show on the plaque under each poster.
music.json     your own songs (mp3/m4a links). They play through the reacting lights.
               ["https://.../song1.mp3", {"src":"song2.mp3","title":"My track"}]
cinema.json    films for the home cinema (mp4 links).
               ["https://.../film1.mp4", {"src":"film2.mp4","title":"Reel"}]

Links must allow cross-origin access (CORS). Files you upload into this room/ folder always work.
Leave a list as [] to use the built-in art, synth music and visualiser.
