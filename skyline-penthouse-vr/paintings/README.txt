FRAMES (list.json) - 15 frames, filled in this order:
 1 feature wall (centre)  2 left wall (big)  3 left wall (tall)  4-6 feature wall (right/left/far)  7 above sculpture
 8-10 left wall small  11-12 right wall (near terrace)  13-15 right wall (by the arc lamp)
Use file names or direct https links. Videos (.mp4/.webm) work too. Leave a slot empty with null, e.g. ["a.jpg", null, "b.mp4"].
Options per entry: {"src":"clip.mp4","full":false,"sound":true}  (full=false keeps a video inside its frame; sound=true unmutes after a click/trigger)

WALLPAPER (wallpapers.json) - your own tileable images, added after the 6 built-in patterns. Press WALLS (top bar) or Walls on the VR wrist menu to cycle.
  ["pattern1.jpg", {"src":"damask.png","name":"Damask","tile":1.5}]   (tile = metres per repeat)
