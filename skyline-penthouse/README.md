# Skyline Penthouse

A walkable luxury penthouse on the 54th floor with floor-to-ceiling glass over a detailed Toronto skyline (CN Tower, Rogers Centre, lake, islands, highway). 1,500 cars, airliners, a helicopter with a searchlight, boats and birds are all moving, and a live sun, moon and sky run on a time-of-day slider.

- Drag to look, W A S D to walk, scroll or Z to zoom, walk out through the glass door to the terrace.
- Tour: guided walkthrough. Lights: interior lights. Run clock: time flows. Performance: lower resolution and no shadows.
- On a phone: drag to look and use the on-screen joystick.

Single static page; Three.js r128 is bundled in `vendor/` (MIT, license included). For the Meta Quest / WebXR version see `../skyline-penthouse-vr/`.
