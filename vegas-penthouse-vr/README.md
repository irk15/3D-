# Vegas Penthouse VR (Meta Quest 3 / WebXR)

The same walkable luxury penthouse as `../skyline-penthouse-vr-plus/`, moved to the 54th floor over the **Las Vegas Strip**. Open it in the **Meta Quest Browser**, pick a quality preset and tap **Enter VR**. It also works as a normal walkable page on desktop and phones.

## What is outside the glass
- **The Strip**: a wide boulevard lined with neon-crowned resort towers, with a smaller downtown cluster far to the north and low desert sprawl beyond.
- **Landmarks**: a Stratosphere-style tower (observation pod, red beacons), **the Sphere** (animated LED shell that cycles between a nebula, a giant eye that looks back at the penthouse, and a spinning planet), a pyramid with a sky beam, a half-scale Eiffel replica, a slowly turning observation wheel, and a lake with dancing fountains.
- **Night life**: sweeping searchlights, animated LED billboards, glowing road along the Strip.
- **Desert and mountains**: sand and scrub instead of a lake, with red-rock ridges to the west and north.
- Still moving: 1,500 cars (30% of them on the Strip), airliners, a tour helicopter with a searchlight, birds by day.

Use the time presets (Sunrise, Midday, Golden hour, Blue hour, Night, Deep night) or the wrist menu in VR to see it at different hours. Night is the showpiece.

## Controls
Same as the other penthouse pages: drag to look and W A S D to walk on desktop; left stick walks, right stick turns or teleports, left wrist menu in VR. See `../skyline-penthouse-vr/README.md` for the full VR table, quality presets and comfort options.

## Notes
- Built from `skyline-penthouse-vr-plus` by swapping the city, landmarks, ground and sky; the interior, vault room, robot, sound and VR layer are unchanged.
- Three.js r128 is bundled in `vendor/` (MIT, license included). Paintings, posters, music and film lists use the same `paintings/` and `room/` files.
- No real brand names or logos are used; the landmarks are generic shapes inspired by the Strip.
