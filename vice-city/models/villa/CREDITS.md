# Casa Flamingo credits

The villa itself (architecture, built-in furniture, textures, baked lighting) was modelled procedurally in Blender for this project.

Furniture comes from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets), used under CC-BY 4.0 / CC0:

- A Beautiful Game: Academy Software Foundation sample, CC-BY 4.0
- Diffuse Transmission Teacup, Glass Vase with Flowers: public domain, CC0
